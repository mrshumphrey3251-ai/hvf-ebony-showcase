# MULTI-CROP GLI CALIBRATION METHODOLOGY
## Sovereign Algorithmic Expansion

Humphrey Virtual Farms is expanding our deterministic Green Leaf Index (GLI) inference engine beyond our temperate corn baseline to include Soy, Wheat, and Specialty crops (e.g., Hops). 

To guarantee absolute precision, we enforce a strict ground-truth calibration protocol for all new crop variants before edge-deployment is authorized.

### 1. The Validation Standard
Every new crop algorithm must be physically cross-validated against field-verified, gravimetric, and optical Leaf Area Index (LAI) ground-truth samples.

### 2. Operational Thresholds
* **Minimum Sample Size:** 500 physically verified field samples per crop variant.
* **Target RMSE:** Root Mean Square Error (RMSE) must mathematically verify at <= 0.08.

We do not guess crop vigor. We measure, calibrate, and dominate the edge.
