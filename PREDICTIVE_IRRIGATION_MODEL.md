# PREDICTIVE IRRIGATION & EVAPOTRANSPIRATION MODEL
## Proactive Agronomic Architecture

Reactive irrigation guarantees temporary crop stress. Humphrey Virtual Farms operates on a strict predictive moisture modeling standard to maintain perfect Volumetric Water Content (VWC) across all managed acreage.

### 1. Evapotranspiration Forecasting
Our sovereign engines do not just read current soil states; they calculate a rolling 24-hour moisture depletion trajectory. By fusing localized dielectric sensor telemetry with meteorological data (temperature spikes, solar radiation intensity), we calculate the exact percentage drop in soil moisture before it occurs.

### 2. Preemptive Action Triggers
If the predicted 24-hour VWC falls below our critical baseline (e.g., 28.0%), the system automatically dispatches preemptive irrigation commands. The crop is watered before physiological stress can manifest.
