# PROJECT EBONY: SMART CITY TRAFFIC & GRID AUTONOMY
## Algorithmic Urban Infrastructure Architecture

Static urban infrastructure paralyzes economic efficiency and threatens public safety. Humphrey Virtual Farms scales its autonomous drone and logistics routing matrices to control metropolitan traffic grids with absolute mathematical authority.

### 1. Dynamic Congestion Flushing
Project Ebony replaces static traffic light timers with dynamic edge processing. By ingesting real-time vehicle density telemetry across the city grid, the Sovereign Matrix autonomously adjusts phase timings to flush arterial congestion, maximizing economic throughput and eliminating arbitrary gridlock.

### 2. Zero-Latency Emergency Corridors
In the event of a medical, fire, or police dispatch, the matrix enforces a Sovereign Override. The system mathematically tracks the incoming velocity and vector of the emergency vehicle, locking all cross-traffic to red and opening a continuous green corridor to the destination. We guarantee first responders reach the crisis with zero infrastructure friction.
