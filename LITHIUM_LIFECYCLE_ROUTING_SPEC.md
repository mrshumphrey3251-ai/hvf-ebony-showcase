# LITHIUM-ION DEGRADATION & LIFECYCLE ROUTING
## Autonomous Battery Health Management

Lithium-ion power cells degrade exponentially. A battery displaying a 100% voltage charge at 300 cycles possesses significantly less true capacity than a new cell. Humphrey Virtual Farms mitigates this physical reality through algorithmic lifecycle routing.

### 1. Algorithmic Capacity Tracking
The Sovereign Command Matrix dynamically tracks the total charge cycles of every drone battery in the fleet. It applies a mathematical degradation curve to calculate the true health capacity of the cell, neutralizing the risk of false-positive voltage readings.

### 2. Dynamic Flight Restriction & Retirement
As a battery degrades, the Command Matrix autonomously shrinks the drone's maximum dispatch radius and flight time window. Once a cell degrades past the 75% critical threshold, the matrix permanently locks it out of flight operations, eliminating mid-mission power failures and securing hardware longevity.
