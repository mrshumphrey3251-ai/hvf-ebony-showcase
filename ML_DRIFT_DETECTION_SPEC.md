# ML CONCEPT DRIFT & AUTONOMOUS AUDITING
## Sovereign AI Quality Control Architecture

Predictive agricultural models are highly susceptible to Concept Drift—the statistical degradation of accuracy caused by shifting environmental baselines (e.g., climate change, new pest variants). Humphrey Virtual Farms does not allow algorithms to operate unmonitored.

### 1. Continuous MAPE Benchmarking
Our Sovereign Command Matrix continuously cross-references all historical yield and irrigation predictions against the actual ground-truth data collected post-harvest. We calculate the Mean Absolute Percentage Error (MAPE) for every neural network deployed in our ecosystem.

### 2. Autonomous Retraining Triggers
If any model's predictive error drifts above our strict 5.0% enterprise threshold, the system autonomously flags the drift, locks the model from issuing active prescriptions, and triggers a data-pipeline refresh for immediate retraining. Our intelligence is perpetually self-correcting.
