# THERMAL CANOPY STRESS & IRRIGATION OPTIMIZATION
## Dual-Sensor Fusion (TIR + VWC)

Traditional agriculture relies entirely on ground-level soil moisture sensors. Humphrey Virtual Farms considers this incomplete. A plant with damaged roots or experiencing extreme atmospheric vapor pressure deficit will suffer acute physiological stress even in wet soil.

### 1. Crop Water Stress Index (CWSI)
Our aerial drone swarms deploy Thermal Infrared (TIR) sensors to capture live canopy temperatures. This is mathematically fused with ambient air temperatures. If the canopy temperature exceeds the ambient air temperature by a critical threshold, it indicates the plant has halted transpiration.

### 2. Autonomous Microclimate Cooling
When acute thermal stress is detected, our Sovereign Command Matrix overrides the soil moisture baseline and triggers emergency micro-irrigation. This autonomous intervention drops the localized ambient temperature, preventing irreversible cellular damage to the crop.
