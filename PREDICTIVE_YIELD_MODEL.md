# PREDICTIVE YIELD ESTIMATION ENGINE
## Telemetry-to-Revenue Financial Forecasting

At Humphrey Virtual Farms, edge data is only valuable if it drives enterprise Return on Investment (ROI). We do not just monitor crop health; we calculate financial futures.

### 1. Algorithmic Yield Fusion
Our predictive models continuously fuse Green Leaf Index (GLI) canopy vigor averages with soil moisture stability matrices. By evaluating these stress factors against the maximum genetic potential of the crop (Bushels per Acre), we generate highly accurate yield projections months before the combine hits the field.

### 2. Live Revenue Modeling
Yield estimates are dynamically multiplied by live commodity market pricing, allowing executive command to forecast Gross Revenue per Acre. This translates agricultural telemetry directly into board-level financial intelligence.
