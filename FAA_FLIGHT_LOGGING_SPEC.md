# FAA AUTONOMOUS FLIGHT TELEMETRY VAULT
## Beyond Visual Line of Sight (BVLOS) Compliance Architecture

Operating a highly kinetic drone swarm across enterprise acreage requires absolute regulatory transparency. Humphrey Virtual Farms secures its FAA waivers by enforcing a zero-trust, immutable flight logging protocol for every airborne asset.

### 1. Autonomous Airspace Enforcement
Every flight is computationally audited against Federal Aviation Administration (FAA) Part 107 regulations. If an asset exceeds the 400-foot Above Ground Level (AGL) hard-deck due to collision evasion or thermal updrafts, the Sovereign Command Matrix autonomously flags the anomaly and adjusts fleet hard-deck parameters for future operations.

### 2. Cryptographic Flight Sealing
To eliminate the liability of manual logbook tampering, every completed flight trajectory, duration, and peak altitude is injected into a cryptographic hashing algorithm. The resulting SHA-256 fingerprint guarantees that our airspace telemetry is 100% accurate, immutable, and immediately presentable for federal audit.
