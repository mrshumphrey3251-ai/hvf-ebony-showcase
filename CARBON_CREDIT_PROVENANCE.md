# CARBON SEQUESTRATION & SUPPLY CHAIN PROVENANCE
## Algorithmic Carbon Credit Verification

Humphrey Virtual Farms translates sustainable agricultural practices into verified, auditable carbon credits. We do not rely on estimates; we utilize a deterministic cryptographic ledger to prove our Net Carbon Capture to enterprise buyers.

### 1. Algorithmic Sequestration Tracking
Our Sovereign Command Matrix tracks every gallon of diesel burned by autonomous machinery and every kilowatt-hour consumed by our drone swarm. These operational emissions are mathematically subtracted from the gross carbon sequestered in our soil via no-till and precision agriculture practices.

### 2. Market-Ready Provenance
The resulting Net Carbon Capture score is generated automatically per sector, providing tier-one corporate buyers with the mathematical provenance required to purchase our carbon credits on the global commodity markets. We farm data and carbon as efficiently as we farm crops.
