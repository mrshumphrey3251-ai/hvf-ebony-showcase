# SOIL TELEMETRY DENSITY STANDARD
## High-Resolution Dielectric Grid

Humphrey Virtual Farms does not extrapolate critical water data over vast acreage. To ensure absolute precision in our agronomic models and autonomous irrigation commands, we enforce a strict hardware density standard across all managed land.

### 1. The 100-Meter Mandate
* **Grid Resolution:** A maximum spacing of 100 meters between LoRaWAN dielectric soil-moisture nodes.
* **Density Ratio:** Minimum of one active telemetry node per hectare.

### 2. Operational Impact
This standard eliminates interpolation blind spots, guaranteeing that our Twin-Brain architecture processes deterministic, hyper-local water volumetric data rather than relying on regional statistical guesses.
