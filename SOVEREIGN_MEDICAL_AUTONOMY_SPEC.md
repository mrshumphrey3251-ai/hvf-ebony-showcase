# PROJECT EBONY: SOVEREIGN MEDICAL TRIAGE & BIOMETRIC AUTONOMY
## Predictive Healthcare and Preventative Dispatch Architecture

The reactive nature of modern healthcare relies on humans recognizing symptoms and seeking help after a critical event has already begun. Humphrey Virtual Farms abstracts its predictive hardware lifecycle algorithms directly into human physiological monitoring to eliminate this latency.

### 1. Continuous Biometric Edge Telemetry
Project Ebony processes live, continuous telemetry from secure biometric wearables. We treat human vitals (SpO2, Heart Rate Variability) with the same mathematical rigor we apply to aviation telemetry. Strict physiological baselines are monitored in real-time by the Sovereign Command Matrix.

### 2. Autonomous Preventative Dispatch
If a patient's vitals demonstrate a critical deviation indicative of imminent respiratory or cardiac failure, the matrix does not wait for a 911 call. It autonomously upgrades the patient's triage status and executes a secure routing command to emergency medical services (EMS), intercepting the medical anomaly before it becomes a fatality. We secure human capital with zero-latency precision.
