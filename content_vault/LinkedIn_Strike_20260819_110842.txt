**The Reality of Leadership: Shop Floor vs. Boardroom**

On the shop floor, leadership is measured in real‑time decisions, safety, and the ability to turn a single line‑stop into a learning moment. In the boardroom, leadership is judged by strategic vision, capital allocation, and the capacity to scale those front‑line lessons into sustainable growth.

The truth: **Effective CEOs bridge both worlds.** They walk the production line, understand the constraints of the crew, and then translate those insights into policies that protect margins, accelerate innovation, and drive shareholder value. When leaders remain siloed, they lose the feedback loop that fuels true competitive advantage.
