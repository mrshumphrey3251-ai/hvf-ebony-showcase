**Leadership: Shop‑Floor Realities vs Boardroom Visions**

The boardroom charts strategy; the shop floor tests it.  
- **Execution beats intention:** A plan is only as strong as the hands that build it.  
- **Visibility is power:** Leaders who walk the line see bottlenecks before they become crises.  
- **Data without context is noise:** On the floor, metrics are lived, not just reported.  
- **Decisions demand accountability:** In the office you delegate; on the line you own the outcome.  
- **Culture is forged in the trenches:** Trust is earned by rolling up sleeves, not by polished presentations.

True leadership bridges both worlds—turning strategic vision into operational excellence, every single day.
