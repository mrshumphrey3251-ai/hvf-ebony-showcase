**Memo**  

**To:** All Employees, Managers, and Contractors  
**From:** The Office of the CEO  
**Date:** August 19 2026  
**Subject:** Zero‑Tolerance Security Policy – Immediate Enforcement  

Effective immediately, the organization adopts a zero‑tolerance stance on all security violations. Every breach—whether intentional, negligent, or accidental—will trigger swift, non‑negotiable consequences.

**Key Directives**

1. **Mandatory Compliance** – All personnel must follow approved security protocols without exception.  
2. **Immediate Reporting** – Any suspected or confirmed incident must be reported to the Security Operations Center within five minutes of detection.  
3. **Consequences** – Violations result in disciplinary action up to and including termination, legal prosecution, and financial restitution. No appeals will overturn these actions.  
4. **Continuous Monitoring** – Security tools will operate 24/7; any deviation triggers automated alerts and instant review.  
5. **Training Verification** – Complete the required security certification by the end of the current quarter; failure to do so constitutes a policy breach.  

**Responsibility**

- **Managers** must enforce compliance daily, conduct spot audits, and certify that their teams meet all requirements.  
- **Security Team** must maintain real‑time oversight, investigate alerts, and enforce sanctions.  
- **All Employees** must embody a security‑first mindset and act decisively when confronted with threats.

Non‑adherence endangers our assets, reputation, and market position. The organization will not tolerate any lapse. Execute these directives now and maintain unwavering vigilance.

**The CEO**
