## 1. CLOSED-LOOP HARDWARE AUTOMATION

### 1.1 Edge-Node CAN-Bus Interfacing
Autonomous tractors and robotics operate on a zero-trust local network. Waypoints and operational directives are calculated by the Master Node and transmitted directly to the vehicle's CAN-bus via encrypted MURS radio links.

### 1.2 RTK GPS & Dead Reckoning
For sub-inch operational accuracy, the Master Node runs a local RTK (Real-Time Kinematic) base station, providing corrections to the fleet without relying on paid, third-party cellular RTK subscriptions.
