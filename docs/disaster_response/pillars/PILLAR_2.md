# 🚜 PILLAR 2: AGRICULTURAL ASSET & LIVESTOCK TRIAGE
**Document ID:** HVF-EMG-PLR-002
**Classification:** Rapid Evacuation & Asset Preservation

---

## 1. LIVESTOCK EVACUATION LOGISTICS

### 1.1 Priority Matrix

*   **Tier 1:** Equine and breeding stock. Load immediately.
*   **Tier 2:** Heavy livestock (cattle, swine). Open all internal gates if transport is unavailable. Never cut exterior perimeter fences near highways (prevents vehicle collisions).
*   **Tier 3:** Poultry and small livestock.

### 1.2 Identification & Tracking

*   **Action 1:** Paint phone numbers directly on livestock flanks using livestock marker or high-visibility spray paint.
*   **Action 2:** Secure halters and lead ropes. Do not use nylon halters (melt risk); strictly use leather or cotton.

---

## 2. HEAVY MACHINERY PRESERVATION

### 2.1 Equipment Staging

*   Move tractors, combines, and fuel bowsers to the center of the largest dirt or gravel clearing (minimum 100-foot buffer from vegetation).
*   Leave keys IN the ignition of all staged equipment to allow rapid relocation by emergency responders.
