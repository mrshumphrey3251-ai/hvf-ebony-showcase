# 🛡️ PILLAR 4: PERIMETER DEFENSE & FIREBREAKS
**Document ID:** HVF-EMG-PLR-004
**Classification:** Structural Hardening

---

## 1. DEFENSIBLE SPACE ENFORCEMENT

### 1.1 Zone 1 (0-30 Feet from Structures)

*   Remove all dead vegetation, pine needles, and leaves from roofs and gutters.
*   Move all firewood piles, propane tanks, and flammable chemicals outside this zone.
*   Disconnect and stage all garden hoses; attach nozzles and leave them visible for firefighters.

### 1.2 Zone 2 (30-100 Feet from Structures)

*   Create horizontal spacing between shrubs and trees to prevent fire bridging.
*   Mow dried grass to a maximum height of 4 inches.

---

## 2. WATER DEPLOYMENT

*   Stage portable gas-powered water pumps at all agricultural ponds or large water troughs.
*   Ensure all pump engines have full fuel tanks and spare spark plugs taped directly to the casing.
