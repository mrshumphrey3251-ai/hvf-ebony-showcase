# ⚕️ PILLAR 3: OFFLINE MEDICAL & BURN TRIAGE
**Document ID:** HVF-EMG-PLR-003
**Classification:** Field Medicine

---

## 1. SMOKE INHALATION & RESPIRATORY CARE

### 1.1 Immediate Field Response

*   Move personnel to an upwind, low-smoke zone immediately.
*   Administer supplemental oxygen if available in the FOB medical kit.
*   Do NOT induce coughing if the airway is severely inflamed.

### 1.2 Burn Triage Protocol

*   **Action 1:** Stop the burning process. Smother flames, remove smoldering clothing (unless adhered to skin).
*   **Action 2:** Cool the burn with clean, lukewarm water (not ice) for 15-20 minutes.
*   **Action 3:** Cover with a dry, sterile, non-adherent dressing to prevent infection.

---

## 2. WATER & HYDRATION

*   Boil all local water sources if the municipal grid loses pressure or contamination is suspected. (Rolling boil for 1 full minute).
