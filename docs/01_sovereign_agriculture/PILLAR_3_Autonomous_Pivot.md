## 1. MISSION STATEMENT
**Objective:** Convert massive center-pivot irrigation rigs into mathematically governed robotic arms, applying precise water volumes dynamically across varied terrain.

---
## 2. ZERO-TO-CEO OVERVIEW
Center pivots usually run at a static speed, over-watering low spots and under-watering hills. Ebony takes control of the pivot's drive motors, modulating the kinetic speed of the rig based on the Dielectric Soil Mesh data, creating a variable-rate application (VRA) that treats every square foot as a unique prescription.

---
## 3. TIER-1 TECHNICAL SCHEMATIC
### 3.1 Pivot Velocity & Application Calculus
Ebony calculates the required kinetic velocity of the pivot ($v_p$) based on target application depth ($D_a$) and system flow rate ($Q$):

$$v_p = \frac{Q}{D_a \cdot L_{sys}}$$
