## 3.0 LOCALIZED MESH NETWORK TOPOLOGY (UWB)
Civilian Wi-Fi cannot cover a farm, and cellular data introduces third-party risk. We utilize a localized Ultra-Wideband (UWB) and Long-Range (LoRa) mesh topology.

*   **Frequency & Bandwidth:** The mesh operates on a heavily encrypted, proprietary frequency block. It utilizes UDP packets for high-bandwidth drone video (ignoring dropped packets to maintain real-time speed) and TCP packets for critical valve commands (guaranteeing delivery).
*   **Node Redundancy:** Every autonomous tractor, drone docking station, and subterranean valve acts as a signal repeater. If a storm knocks out a radio tower, the mesh instantly heals itself, bouncing signals off the tractors to maintain 100% perimeter coverage.

## 4.0 CRITICAL FAILOVER PROTOCOLS (THE KINETIC GUILLOTINE)
A sovereign system must know how to fail safely.

*   **Power Severance:** If the local power grid is destroyed, the Master Edge Node instantly falls back to localized battery backups and signals the Energy Vertical (Vertical 4) to spool up onsite generators. All non-critical drone flights are grounded to conserve power for the water valves.
*   **Adversarial Breach:** If a hostile entity attempts to plug a physical laptop into a water valve actuator in the field, the Edge Node detects the unrecognized MAC address. It instantly triggers the **Kinetic Guillotine**, severing that valve from the mesh, killing its power, and scrambling the defense swarm to the exact GPS coordinates of the breach.
*   **Catastrophic Core Failure:** In the mathematically improbable event the Master Edge Node hardware is completely destroyed, all physical valves default to a "Closed" mechanical state to prevent flooding and resource dumping.
