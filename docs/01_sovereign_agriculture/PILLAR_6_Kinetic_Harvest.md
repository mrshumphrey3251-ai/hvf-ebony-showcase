## 3. TIER-1 TECHNICAL SCHEMATIC
### 3.1 Harvest Efficiency Calculus
Ebony optimizes the kinetic speed of the harvester ($V_h$) based on the maximum internal threshing capacity ($C_{max}$) and the physical crop density ($\rho_c$):

$$V_h = \frac{C_{max}}{\rho_c \cdot W_{header}}$$
