## 1. MISSION STATEMENT
**Objective:** Execute variable-rate injection of Nitrogen, Phosphorus, and Potassium (NPK) directly into the hydro-grid based on instantaneous crop demand.

---
## 2. ZERO-TO-CEO OVERVIEW
Broadcasting dry fertilizer is inefficient and ecologically disastrous. Ebony controls industrial peristaltic pumps to inject liquid nutrients directly into the irrigation water (fertigation). The chemical mix is altered dynamically based on the drone swarm's GLI telemetry.

---
## 3. TIER-1 TECHNICAL SCHEMATIC
### 3.1 Injection Mass Flow Calculus
Ebony calculates the required nutrient injection rate ($q_{inj}$) to achieve the target concentration ($C_t$) in the main water line ($Q_{main}$):

$$q_{inj} = \frac{C_t \cdot Q_{main}}{C_{stock}}$$
