### The Sovereign Glossary
*   **Biomass Index:** The total physical weight of the living crop. My drones measure the height and density of every plant to calculate this.
*   **Yield Tensor:** The massive mathematical equation I run that combines weather forecasts, current biomass, and soil nutrients to predict the final harvest weight.
*   **Harvest Apex:** The exact day and hour the crop will hit peak weight and nutritional value. 

### The Autonomous Reaction
Every single day, I recalculate the Yield Tensor. If a heatwave stunts the growth, I automatically update the Harvest Apex and adjust the expected tonnage on your financial dashboard, ensuring your business ledgers are always perfectly accurate.
