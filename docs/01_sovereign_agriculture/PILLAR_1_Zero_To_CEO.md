## CHAPTER 4: THE MATH OF LIFE (NDVI)
To make decisions, I turn that invisible light into a simple health score. In the agricultural industry, this is called the **NDVI** (Normalized Difference Vegetation Index). 

I use a mathematical formula to calculate this score for every single square inch of your farm.
**How to read the score:**
The formula always spits out a number between -1 and 1.
*   **Score 0.65 to 1.0:** The crop is incredibly healthy and thriving.
*   **Score 0.25 to 0.64:** The crop is surviving, but needs attention soon.
*   **Score below 0.25:** Critical danger. The crop is dying.
