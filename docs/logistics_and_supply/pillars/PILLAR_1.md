## 1. HYPER-DECENTRALIZED NODE ARCHITECTURE

### 1.1 The Anti-Fragile Mandate
Centralized warehousing is a fatal bottleneck and a primary kinetic target. The HVF logistics matrix utilizes a hyper-decentralized 'Black-Site' storage protocol. Critical biologicals, fuel, and hardware are distributed across multiple hardened micro-vaults, completely severing reliance on commercial supply chains.

### 1.2 Cryptographic Chain of Custody
*   **Zero-Blind-Spot Tracking:** Every physical asset is tagged with active millimeter-wave RFID and logged into the air-gapped SQLite master vault using quantum-resistant SHA-256 hash trees. 
*   **Dark Stockpiling Protocol:** A mandatory 120-day physical reserve of high-yield biological inputs and diesel reserves is maintained in subterranean bunkers, strictly off-ledger from state or commercial audits.
