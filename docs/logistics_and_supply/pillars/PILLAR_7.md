## 2. BIO-DIESEL SYNTHESIS & CLOSED-LOOP FUEL

### 2.1 On-Site Transesterification
The platform oversees on-site bio-diesel refinement units, continuously recycling agricultural byproducts (lipids, oils) through an automated chemical transesterification process into high-grade heavy machinery fuel.
*   **The Reaction:** Triglycerides are reacted with methanol and a localized catalyst (lye) to produce Fatty Acid Methyl Esters (FAME), permanently eliminating the need for commercial diesel procurement.
*   **Fuel Stabilization:** Synthesized fuel is automatically injected with biocides and stabilizing agents to prevent microbial degradation, ensuring a minimum 24-month shelf life in subterranean, climate-locked storage vaults.
