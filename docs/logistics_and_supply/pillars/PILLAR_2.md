## 1. TACTICAL MOBILITY & ISOLATION PROTOCOLS

### 1.1 Heavy Machinery as Tactical Armor
Agricultural heavy machinery (tractors, combines, heavy loaders) constitutes the tactical armor of the HVF Empire. They must operate flawlessly in hostile, post-grid, and contaminated environments.

*   **Faraday Isolation:** All primary diagnostic modules, backup Engine Control Modules (ECMs), and proprietary comms arrays are housed in military-spec, grounded Faraday enclosures rated for 80dB attenuation of broadband electromagnetic frequencies.
*   **CBRN Overpressure Cabs:** Operator cabins are sealed and pressurized to 0.15 PSI above atmospheric pressure. Air intake is routed through modular, military-grade HEPA and activated charcoal filtration units (MERV 17 equivalent), ensuring operator survival in chemically or biologically compromised operational zones.

## 2. OFFLINE NAVIGATION & KINETIC EVASION

### 2.1 Satellite-Denied Navigation
Global Positioning Systems (GPS) are highly susceptible to spoofing, jamming, and orbital kinetic strikes. The HVF fleet operates entirely independent of the satellite constellation.

*   **Inertial Navigation Systems (INS):** Fleet vehicles are equipped with localized INS (fiber-optic gyroscopes and accelerometers). If GPS deviation exceeds 3 meters or a jammer is detected, the fleet auto-switches to INS.
*   **Encrypted Mesh Telemetry:** Navigation data is synchronized across the fleet via an encrypted 900MHz / 2.4GHz mesh network, self-healing and bouncing off deployed edge nodes.

### 2.2 Swarm Routing AI
*   **Dynamic Threat Avoidance:** The master console continuously calculates tertiary extraction and resupply routes. The algorithm is weighted to actively avoid major highways, commercial checkpoints, bridges, and AI-predicted kinetic threat vectors based on real-time acoustic/thermal telemetry.
