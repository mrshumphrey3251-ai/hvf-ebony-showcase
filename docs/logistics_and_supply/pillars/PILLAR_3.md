## 2. EMISSION CONTROL (EMCON) & DARK FLIGHT PROTOCOLS

### 2.1 Complete Spectral Masking
Aerial logistics must remain invisible to commercial radar, thermal imaging, and RF triangulation.
*   **Thermal Bloom Reduction:** High-discharge lithium-polymer battery cores and brushless ESCs (Electronic Speed Controllers) are wrapped in aerogel blankets, heavily dampening the infrared (IR) signature visible to hostile FLIR systems.
*   **Acoustic Masking:** Logistics drones are deployed utilizing terrain-following radar algorithms and nap-of-the-earth (NOE) routing (maintaining altitudes below 50 feet AGL). This uses ground clutter to minimize acoustic propagation and radar cross-sections.

### 2.2 RF Silence & Telemetry
*   **ADS-B Severance:** Aerial freight operates strictly 'dark.' ADS-B (Automatic Dependent Surveillance-Broadcast) transponders are physically disconnected from the main power rail under executive protocol. 
*   **Directional Bursts:** Flight telemetry is transmitted exclusively via heavily encrypted, directional millimeter-wave RF bursts, requiring exact line-of-sight to edge nodes and rendering it invisible to omnidirectional commercial scanners.

## 3. PRECISION PAYLOAD EXFILTRATION

### 3.1 Automated Drop Sequencing
*   **Payload Coordination:** High-value payloads (medical trauma kits, critical micro-manufacturing parts, specialized biologicals) are prioritized for UAV deployment to Forward Operating Bases (FOBs).
*   **Spectral Landing Zones:** Drop zones are designated dynamically by the Master Console using encrypted, modulated infrared (IR) strobes operating at 940nm, invisible to the naked eye but blindingly clear to the drone's multispectral optical payload. 
*   **Magnetic Uncoupling:** Drones execute an autonomous corkscrew descent, hover at 3 feet AGL, uncouple the payload via electromagnetic latches, and exfiltrate the drop zone at a 45-degree ascent vector in under 15 seconds, requiring absolutely zero human intervention.
