## 1. VERTICAL TACTICAL LOGISTICS (VTOL)

### 1.1 Autonomous Blackout Resupply
When ground corridors are compromised, logistics shift to the airspace.
*   **Acoustic Masking:** Logistics drones are deployed utilizing terrain-following radar algorithms and nap-of-the-earth (NOE) routing.
*   **Dark Flight Telemetry:** Aerial freight operates strictly 'dark.' Telemetry is transmitted exclusively via heavily encrypted, directional millimeter-wave RF.
*   **Spectral Payload Drops:** Landing zones are designated dynamically using encrypted infrared (IR) strobes. Drones execute autonomous descent and uncouple payloads in under 15 seconds.
