## 1. VERTICAL TACTICAL LOGISTICS (VTOL) & DARK FLIGHT

### 1.1 Autonomous Blackout Resupply
When ground corridors are compromised by kinetic action, flooding, or grid collapse, all critical logistics immediately shift to the airspace via heavy-lift, multi-rotor autonomous platforms.

*   **Acoustic & Thermal Masking:** Logistics drones are deployed utilizing terrain-following radar algorithms and nap-of-the-earth (NOE) routing (maintaining altitudes below 50 feet AGL). This minimizes radar cross-sections and acoustic signatures.
*   **EMCON (Emission Control) Dark Flight:** Aerial freight operates strictly 'dark.' ADS-B (Automatic Dependent Surveillance-Broadcast) transponders are hard-disabled under executive override. 
*   **Directional Telemetry:** Flight telemetry is transmitted exclusively via heavily encrypted, directional millimeter-wave RF, requiring exact line-of-sight to edge nodes, rendering it invisible to omnidirectional commercial scanners.

## 2. PRECISION PAYLOAD DELIVERY

### 2.1 Automated Exfiltration Drops
*   **Payload Coordination:** High-value, low-weight payloads (medical trauma kits, critical micro-manufacturing parts, specialized biologicals) are prioritized for UAV deployment to Forward Operating Bases (FOBs).
*   **Spectral Landing Zones:** Drop zones are designated dynamically by the Master Console using encrypted, modulated infrared (IR) strobes visible only to the drone's multispectral optical payload. 
*   **Execution Timeline:** Drones execute autonomous descent, uncouple the payload via magnetic latches, and exfiltrate the drop zone in under 15 seconds, requiring zero human intervention and minimizing loiter vulnerability.
