## 1. ON-SITE COMPONENT REPLICATION

### 1.1 Severing the Hardware Tether
HVF does not wait on international shipping for critical replacement parts during a crisis.
*   **Industrial 3D Forging:** The edge nodes integrate directly with on-site CNC mills and industrial carbon-fiber/metal 3D printers. 
*   **Digital Schematic Vault:** The offline SQLite vault contains thousands of proprietary and reverse-engineered CAD blueprints for drone rotors, tractor linkage pins, and sensor housings, enabling instant fabrication.
