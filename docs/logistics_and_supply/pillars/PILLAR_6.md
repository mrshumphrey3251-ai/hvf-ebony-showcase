## 1. ON-SITE COMPONENT REPLICATION

### 1.1 Severing the Hardware Tether
HVF does not wait on international shipping, commercial logistics, or foreign manufacturing for critical replacement parts during a supply chain crisis. The empire is self-forging.

*   **Industrial Fabrication:** Edge nodes integrate directly with on-site, off-grid CNC (Computer Numerical Control) mills and industrial 3D printers capable of extruding PEEK, carbon-fiber reinforced nylon, and aerospace-grade aluminum.
*   **Tolerance Specifications:** Machining operations are calibrated to maintain tolerances of +/- 0.005mm for engine linkages, drone rotors, and sensor housings.

### 1.2 Digital Schematic Vault & Quality Control
*   **Air-Gapped CAD Library:** The offline SQLite vault contains thousands of proprietary and reverse-engineered CAD blueprints, allowing instant fabrication of mission-critical components without an internet connection.
*   **Optical QC Scanning:** All freshly forged parts are subjected to an automated 3D optical scan, overlaying the physical product against the digital schematic. Variances result in immediate recycling of the part.
