## 1. ROUTE SECURITY & ASSET PROTECTION

### 1.1 Overwatch Integration
High-value freight moving beyond the primary perimeter requires active tactical overwatch.
*   **UAV Vanguard Escort:** Swarm drones deploy 2 miles ahead of transport convoys, feeding real-time threat assessments directly to the operator's HUD.
*   **Automated Choke-Point Identification:** The AI Core algorithmically spaces convoys to ensure the entire asset train cannot be trapped in a single terrain choke-point.

### 1.2 Counter-Ambush Execution
*   **Kinetic Evasion Maneuvers:** If a threat is detected, heavy machinery acts as a localized barricade while high-speed transport assets exfiltrate the zone.
