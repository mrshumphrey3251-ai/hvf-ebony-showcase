## 1. ROUTE SECURITY & VANGUARD OVERWATCH

### 1.1 Tactical Convoy Spacing & Identification
High-value freight moving beyond the primary hardened perimeter requires active, militarized tactical overwatch to prevent seizure and ambush.

*   **Algorithmic Spacing:** The AI Core calculates optimal convoy spacing (typically 150 to 300 meters) to ensure the entire asset train cannot be trapped simultaneously in a single terrain choke-point.
*   **Automated 'Red Zone' Identification:** The matrix pre-identifies bridges, narrow embankments, and elevation disadvantages as 'Red Zones.' Convoy speed and spacing are dynamically adjusted prior to entering these zones.

### 1.2 UAV Vanguard Escort
*   **Forward Reconnaissance:** Swarm drones equipped with radiometric thermal and high-zoom optical payloads deploy exactly 2 miles ahead of transport convoys. 
*   **Real-Time HUD Feed:** Threat assessments, thermal anomalies, and barricade detections are fed in real-time directly to the transport operator's HUD and the CEO's Master Console.

## 2. COUNTER-AMBUSH EXECUTION

### 2.1 Kinetic Evasion Maneuvers
*   **Localized Barricading:** If a kinetic threat is detected, heavy machinery (bulldozers, front-end loaders) accompanying the convoy immediately act as a localized, mobile barricade, shielding high-speed transport assets.
*   **Radio Silence Exfiltration:** In a compromised sector, all broad-spectrum RF comms are instantly severed to prevent triangulation. Assets revert to pre-programmed rally coordinates using solely offline Inertial Navigation Systems (INS).
