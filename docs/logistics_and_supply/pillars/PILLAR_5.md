## 1. INBOUND FREIGHT PURIFICATION & QUARANTINE

### 1.1 Sector Isolation Protocols
All external physical assets, vehicles, and personnel entering the Sovereign Network must undergo exhaustive sterilization to prevent the introduction of aggressive agricultural pathogens, weaponized bio-agents, or chemical fallout.
*   **The Airlock Staging Zone:** Assets are halted at the outer perimeter. No asset proceeds to the inner sanctum without AI clearance validating the decontamination cycle.

### 1.2 Spectrometric & Chemical Identification
Before decontamination begins, inbound organics are swabbed. Mass spectrometers linked to the Edge Node execute a localized spectral analysis, cross-referencing the molecular signature against the [REDACTED_VAULT_DB] threat library to identify specific nerve agents or defoliants.
