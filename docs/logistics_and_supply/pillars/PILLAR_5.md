## 1. INBOUND FREIGHT PURIFICATION

### 1.1 Sector Quarantine
All external physical assets entering the Sovereign Network must undergo sterilization.
*   **UV-C & Ozone Chambers:** High-value logistics drops are quarantined in automated sterilization bays utilizing industrial ozone generators and high-intensity UV-C arrays to neutralize biological contagions before entry.
*   **Chemical Wash-Down:** Heavy transport vehicles undergo an automated, high-pressure neutralizing chemical wash at the outer perimeter gate to remove hazardous fallout or aggressive agricultural pathogens.
