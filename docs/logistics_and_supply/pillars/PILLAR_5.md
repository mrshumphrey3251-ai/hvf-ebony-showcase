## 1. INBOUND FREIGHT PURIFICATION & QUARANTINE

### 1.1 Sector Isolation Protocols
All external physical assets, vehicles, and personnel entering the Sovereign Network must undergo exhaustive sterilization to prevent the introduction of aggressive agricultural pathogens, bio-weapons, or chemical fallout.

*   **Staging & Airlock:** Assets are halted at the outer perimeter 'Airlock' staging zone. No asset proceeds to the inner sanctum without AI clearance.

### 1.2 Multi-Stage Decontamination
*   **High-Intensity UV-C Arrays:** High-value logistics drops and small freight are quarantined in automated sterilization bays. Arrays emitting exact 254nm UV-C wavelengths saturate the payload for a calculated duration, destroying DNA/RNA bonds of pathogens.
*   **Ozone Saturation:** Industrial ozone generators flood the sealed chamber to 20 ppm, ensuring complete gaseous penetration into crevices unreachable by UV-C light.
*   **Chemical Wash-Down:** Heavy transport vehicles undergo an automated, high-pressure neutralizing chemical wash (utilizing precise hypochlorite and surfactant solutions) at the outer gate to remove hazardous radiological dust or chemical agents before clearing the checkpoint.
