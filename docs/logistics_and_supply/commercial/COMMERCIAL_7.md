**Q: Why don't you use standard freight brokers like C.H. Robinson?**
A: Commercial brokers rely on cell phones and public internet. If the grid drops, they lose the trucks. HVF utilizes encrypted 900MHz mesh and offline INS routing. We never lose eyes on the asset.

**Q: What happens if a transport vehicle is hijacked?**
A: The Master Edge Node detects the route deviation instantly. It initiates an encrypted self-destruct of the vehicle's onboard data vault and remotely severs the vehicle's fuel injection CAN-bus, bricking the vehicle in place for tactical recovery.

**Q: Are your heavy-lift drones FAA compliant?**
