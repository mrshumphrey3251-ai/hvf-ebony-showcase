## 1. ASSET SEIZURE & TRIAGE

### 1.1 Triggering Event Protocols
In the event of a catastrophic regional failure, commercial operations cease, and tactical reallocation begins.

*   **Fuel & Water Triage:** Automated suspension of all non-essential agricultural operations. 100% of fuel and water reserves are re-routed to backup generators and critical livestock survival sectors.
*   **Contractual Force Majeure:** The system instantly auto-generates and dispatches legally binding force majeure notifications to all commercial buyers via the encrypted comms deck, halting outbound freight.
