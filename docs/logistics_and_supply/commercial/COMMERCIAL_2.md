## 1. EXECUTIVE EMBARGO & ASSET TRIAGE

### 1.1 Total Embargo Execution
In the event of regional grid collapse or kinetic threat, all commercial operations are instantly terminated. Survival and continuity of operations (COOP) take absolute priority.
*   **Fluid & Power Seizure:** The AI Core executes an automated suspension of all non-essential sectors. 100% of fuel, water, and microgrid power reserves are ruthlessly re-routed to critical survival sectors, backup generators, and server arrays.
*   **Automated Force Majeure:** The system bypasses human legal review, auto-generating and dispatching legally binding *Force Majeure* declarations to all commercial buyers via encrypted dispatch, legally severing outbound freight obligations in milliseconds.
*   **Drone-Enforced Lockdown:** The UAV fleet is immediately retasked from agricultural surveillance to perimeter overwatch, securing all decentralized storage nodes against unauthorized access.
