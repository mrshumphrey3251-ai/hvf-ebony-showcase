## 1. EXECUTIVE EMBARGO & ASSET TRIAGE

### 1.1 Total Embargo Execution
In the event of regional grid collapse, systemic supply chain failure, or kinetic threat, all commercial operations are instantly terminated. Survival and continuity of operations (COOP) take absolute priority.

*   **Fluid & Power Seizure:** The AI Core executes an automated suspension of all non-essential and commercial agricultural sectors. 100% of fuel, water, and microgrid power reserves are ruthlessly re-routed to critical survival sectors, backup generators, and edge-node server arrays.

### 1.2 Automated Force Majeure & Legal Severance
*   **Algorithmic Legal Dispatch:** The system bypasses human legal review. Upon DEFCON 1 activation, the AI Core auto-generates and dispatches legally binding *Force Majeure* declarations to all commercial buyers, vendors, and state entities via encrypted dispatch. 

*   **Obligation Termination:** This action legally severs all outbound freight obligations and financial penalties in milliseconds, protecting the empire from breach-of-contract liabilities during a survival scenario.
