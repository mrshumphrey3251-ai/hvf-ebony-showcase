## 1. ZERO-TRUST VENDOR PROCUREMENT & VALIDATION

### 1.1 Spectrometric Inbound Audits
HVF assumes all third-party inbound freight is compromised until proven otherwise by autonomous hardware.

*   **Automated Payload Sanitization:** Inbound biological inputs are immediately subjected to optical and mass-spectrometry scanning upon perimeter breach to verify chemical composition against the manifest.

*   **Smart-Contract Execution:** Financial transactions are bound to private ledger smart contracts. If the variance of the delivered asset exceeds a strict 0.1% tolerance, the AI Core automatically executes a smart-contract freeze, halting all escrow payments and quarantining the vehicle.

### 1.2 Biometric Gate Custody
*   **Multi-Factor Authorization:** No commercial driver may breach the inner perimeter without a cryptographic handshake (pre-issued digital token) and localized biometric verification (retinal or fingerprint scan) at the physical security checkpoint.
