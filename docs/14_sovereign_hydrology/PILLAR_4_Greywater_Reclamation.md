## 3. TIER-1 TECHNICAL SCHEMATIC
### 3.1 Membrane Flux Calculus
Ebony calculates the membrane filtration flux ($J$) based on the Transmembrane Pressure ($\Delta P$), dynamic fluid viscosity ($\mu$), and total membrane resistance ($R_m$):

$$J = \frac{\Delta P}{\mu \cdot R_m}$$
