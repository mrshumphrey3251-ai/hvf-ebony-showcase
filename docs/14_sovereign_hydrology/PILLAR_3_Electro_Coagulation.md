### 3.1 Faraday's Law of Electrolysis Calculus
Ebony calculates the mass of coagulant generated ($m$) based on current ($I$), time ($t$), molar mass ($M$), valency ($z$), and Faraday's constant ($F$):

$$m = \frac{I \cdot t \cdot M}{z \cdot F}$$
