## 1. STRATEGIC VISION & THE VULNERABILITY OF CLOUD C2
Modern tactical operations are placed in an untenable strategic position: total dependence on centralized cloud hyperscalers. Streaming drone telemetry, unit positions, and threat maps to third-party servers introduces catastrophic failure points, including connectivity blackouts in contested regions and foreign kill-switches.

## 2. THE SOVEREIGN TACTICAL MANDATE
Humphrey Defense Tactical (HDT) operates on a non-negotiable principle: **Operational Dominance Through Absolute Local Sovereignty.**
* **Compute Stays at the FOB:** All neural inference, threat aggregation, and computer vision execute on bare-metal hardware physically owned and secured by the commander.
* **Zero Involuntary Telemetry:** Not a single byte of flight video or radar data leaves the local machine without authenticated cryptographic authorization.
* **Deterministic Survival:** The HDT platform operates indefinitely in complete air-gap conditions without internet connectivity.
