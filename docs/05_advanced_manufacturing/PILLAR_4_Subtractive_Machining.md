## 1. MISSION STATEMENT
**Objective:** Guarantee hardware sovereignty by maintaining the onsite capacity to machine replacement components from raw titanium and aluminum billet, neutralizing supply chain dependency.

---
## 2. ZERO-TO-CEO OVERVIEW
If a structural actuator breaks, waiting for a shipping container from overseas is a vulnerability. The farm possesses heavy 5-axis CNC mills. Ebony generates deterministic G-Code directly from CAD files and feeds it to the mills, carving exact replacement parts from raw metal blocks on demand.
