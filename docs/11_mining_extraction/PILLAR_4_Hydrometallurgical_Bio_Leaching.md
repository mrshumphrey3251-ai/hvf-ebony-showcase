## 2. ZERO-TO-CEO OVERVIEW
Traditional smelting requires massive heat and creates toxic exhaust. HVF uses biology. Raw ore is crushed and deposited into sealed vats. Ebony manages the pH, temperature, and oxygen levels to keep extremophile bacteria alive. These bacteria literally eat the rock, leaving behind liquid solutions of pure copper, lithium, and titanium.
