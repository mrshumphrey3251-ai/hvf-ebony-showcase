## 2. ZERO-TO-CEO OVERVIEW
Subterranean machinery generates massive heat and silica dust. Pumping this dust directly to the surface is a tactical and environmental liability. Ebony governs a closed-loop ventilation matrix, routing tunnel air through localized electrostatic precipitators and water scrubbers, ensuring the exhaust air is pristine before it leaves the sovereign perimeter.
