## 3. TIER-1 TECHNICAL SCHEMATIC
### 3.1 Open Channel Flow Calculus (Manning's Equation)
Ebony determines the flow velocity ($V$) of the storm trenches based on the hydraulic radius ($R_h$), channel slope ($S$), and surface roughness coefficient ($n$):

$$V = \frac{1.49}{n} R_h^{2/3} S^{1/2}$$
