5. **Multi-Tenant Governance & RBAC:** Hierarchical scopes (global -> region -> site -> field).
6. **Automated Regulatory-Compliance Engine:** Set-and-forget compliance layer (CCPA, GDPR).
7. **Scalable Cloud-Native Architecture:** Kubernetes auto-scaling, distributed time-series DB.
8. **Interoperability with FMS:** OpenAPI connectors for Top 5 Farm-Management Systems.
9. **Advanced Prescriptive Analytics:** Reinforcement-learning optimizer for cost-vs-yield.
10. **Transparent Pricing & Consumption Metering:** OPEX transparency, cost per acre/rack.
11. **Community & Ecosystem Programs:** Partner tiers and drone telemetry hackathons.
12. **Continuous Learning & Model Updates:** CI/CD pipeline for nightly model weight pushes.
