# SOVEREIGN MICRO-GRID POWER ROUTING
## Energy Independence & Load Shedding Architecture

Relying on a rural public power grid introduces unacceptable operational vulnerability. Humphrey Virtual Farms mitigates this risk by establishing a fully autonomous, solar-backed edge micro-grid capable of infinite self-sustainment for mission-critical systems.

### 1. Dynamic Load Shedding
When the public grid fails, the Sovereign Command Matrix instantly takes control of the edge power distribution. It algorithmically severs power to non-essential loads (maintenance bays, auxiliary lighting) to preserve localized battery wall reserves.

### 2. Tiered Criticality Routing
Hardware assets are strictly tiered by operational necessity. If battery reserves reach critical thresholds during extended blackouts, all secondary systems (drone charging bays) are immediately halted. 100% of the remaining solar and battery throughput is routed directly to Tier 1 systems: the Sovereign Gateway and the irrigation pumps, ensuring the farm remains online and the crops remain watered under any external failure scenario.
