# ZERO-TRUST EDGE INITIATIVE
## Securing the Physical Perimeter

Humphrey Virtual Farms enforces absolute bare-metal sovereignty. As part of our continuous Phase 1 Foundational Excellence roadmap, all physical field edge devices and gateways operate on a Zero-Trust architecture.

* **Hardware Root of Trust:** Mandatory Trusted Platform Module (TPM) integration.
* **Firmware Integrity:** Strict enforcement of Secure Boot and signed firmware updates.
* **Physical Security:** Tamper-evident hardware enclosures.

We do not simulate safety. We engineer deterministic survival.
