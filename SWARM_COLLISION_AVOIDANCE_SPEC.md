# DRONE SWARM COLLISION AVOIDANCE MATRIX
## Autonomous 3D Spatial Deconfliction

Humphrey Virtual Farms operates a dynamic, multi-agent edge drone swarm. To guarantee hardware safety and regulatory airspace compliance, we enforce a strict autonomous deconfliction matrix.

### 1. 3D Safety Sphere Monitoring
Our Sovereign Command Matrix continuously calculates the 3D Euclidean distance (latitude, longitude, and altitude) between all active aerial nodes. A strict 50-meter spherical safety perimeter is enforced around every drone in the fleet.

### 2. Autonomous Altitude Evasion
In the event of intersecting flight paths—such as an emergency Return-To-Home (RTH) crossing an active telemetry route—the system does not rely on human intervention. Upon breaching the safety sphere, the matrix instantly commands an autonomous altitude deconfliction, forcing one asset to climb and the other to descend, neutralizing the collision vector in milliseconds.
